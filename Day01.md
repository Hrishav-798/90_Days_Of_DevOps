# DevOps Winter Arc — Day 01 of 90 Days of DevOps Challenge

> **Learn • Build • Deploy • Repeat**

---

## 🎯 The 90-Day Commitment

Today marks **Day 01** of my **90 Days of DevOps** journey.

For the next 90 days, I commit to staying disciplined and consistent. No shortcuts and no passive watching—just real hands-on terminal execution, troubleshooting, and building production-grade infrastructure every single day.

## ⚡ The Code of Consistency

1. **Daily Execution:** Show up every day. No zero-progress days.
2. **Terminal First:** Build and troubleshoot directly in the CLI.
3. **Document Everything:** Commit every lab, configuration, and bug fix to this repo.

> *"Consistency beats intensity. 90 days of disciplined execution starts now."*
